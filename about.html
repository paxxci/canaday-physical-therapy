Dr. Tanner Canaday, PT, DPT is a physical therapist dedicated to providing personalized, high-quality care that helps people return to the activities and life they enjoy.

Originally from the Oregon Coast, Tanner attended Southern Oregon University, where he competed as a collegiate wrestler while completing his undergraduate education. He went on to earn his Doctorate in Physical Therapy from Pacific University.

Tanner developed a strong interest in neurological rehabilitation early in his training and spent the majority of his clinical rotations in neurological settings. He has since gained experience treating a wide range of neurological and orthopedic conditions, with a particular interest in concussion and traumatic brain injury, vestibular disorders, stroke recovery, and balance dysfunction.

When he's not working with patients, Tanner's time is centered around his wife and two boys and enjoying the outdoors. Whether hunting, fishing, or spending time along the Oregon Coast, being outside with family is where he feels most at home.
