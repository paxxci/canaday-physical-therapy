Dr. Tanner Canaday, PT, DPT

With a profound passion for restoring mobility and confidence, Dr. Canaday founded Canaday Mobile Physical Therapy to bridge the gap between clinical expertise and patient convenience. He believes that healing happens best in an environment where the patient feels most comfortable—their own home.

His approach is deeply rooted in evidence-based practice and personalized attention. By completely removing the rush of a busy clinic, Dr. Canaday focuses 100% of his time during a session directly on you, leading to faster recovery, better outcomes, and a significantly higher standard of care.
