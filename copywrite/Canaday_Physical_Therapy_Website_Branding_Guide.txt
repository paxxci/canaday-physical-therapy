Brand Vision
Canaday Physical Therapy is a mobile physical therapy practice built around helping people regain confidence and return to the activities that matter most. I specialize in neurological rehabilitation with an emphasis on concussion recovery, vestibular disorders, balance dysfunction, stroke rehabilitation, and complex neurological conditions, while also treating orthopedic injuries.

I want the brand to feel:
• Modern but approachable
• Professional and evidence-based
• Calm, confident, and trustworthy
• Rooted in the outdoors and Southern Oregon
• Personal—not corporate

The mountain imagery represents resilience, overcoming challenges, and returning to the activities people love.

About
At Canaday Physical Therapy, every visit is focused entirely on you.

Unlike busy outpatient clinics, I provide individualized, one-on-one treatment in the comfort of your home, workplace, gym, or other convenient location throughout the Rogue Valley.

My goal is to identify the root cause of your symptoms, develop a personalized treatment plan, and help you regain confidence, independence, and performance—whether that means returning to work, sports, hiking, or simply everyday life.

Why Mobile Physical Therapy?
Receiving care where you live and move allows therapy to be more practical, functional, and personalized.

Benefits include:
• No waiting rooms or crowded clinics
• One-on-one care every visit
• Treatment tailored to your real environment
• Flexible scheduling
• Individualized plans focused on lasting results
My Philosophy
Recovery isn't just about reducing pain—it's about restoring confidence.

Every treatment session is built around meaningful movement, evidence-based care, and helping you return to the activities that make life enjoyable.

Whether you're recovering from a concussion, managing neurological symptoms, or overcoming an orthopedic injury, my goal is to help you move better, feel stronger, and regain the freedom to live the life you want.
